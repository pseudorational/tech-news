## 1. Executive assessment

- **Evidence shifted from financing announcements toward operating results.** Since yesterday’s cutoff, SK hynix and NXP reported stronger sales and profitability; their different end markets argue for examining individual suppliers rather than treating all technology demand as one cycle. See the financial comparison below.
- **The clearest opportunity is infrastructure supply with demonstrated earnings.** SK hynix’s results and NXP’s cash generation provide a firmer analytical starting point than announcements of future capacity alone. This is a business assessment, not a valuation-based purchase recommendation.
- **Competition is widening.** Oracle will distribute Google TPUs beginning in 2027. The potential benefit is greater customer reach; the test is profitable utilization after deployment. [Oracle announcement](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/ai-world-oracle-brings-google-tpus-to-oracle-cloud-infrastructure-2026-10-27/).
- **The main risk remains the gap between commitments and realized returns.** Today’s earnings and monetary-policy catalysts could change financing costs and assumptions about demand. No outcome released after the cutoff is incorporated.

## 6. Industry fundamentals and competition

SK hynix says agreements cover over half of 2027–2028 capacity, while expecting memory undersupply through 2030. These are management’s contracted-coverage statement and market outlook respectively, not an independently verified industry forecast. Contract duration improves visibility but does not eliminate customer, pricing or delivery risk. [SK hynix](https://news.skhynix.com/en/q3-2026-business-results/).

For cloud buyers, the Oracle–Google arrangement could increase bargaining options. Investors should distinguish a competing architecture’s theoretical economics from migration costs, software compatibility and realized workload performance. No independent comparable-cost benchmark is adopted here.

The financing structure also changes how to read demand: a supplier can sell equipment while retaining exposure to its future resale value. Consequently, equipment revenue, customer credit quality and the supplier’s contingent obligations should be evaluated together. This is an analytical implication of the Broadcom disclosure, not a quantified loss estimate.

## 7. Valuation

No peer multiples or price targets are presented: synchronized share prices, diluted capital structures, enterprise values and comparable earnings estimates were not verified. A low multiple on exceptional memory profits can give a misleading impression of cheapness if the denominator normalizes; a high growth rate cannot establish an attractive entry price by itself.

CNBC reports widely divergent **2027 TPU sales estimates: $62.5bn at TD Cowen versus $99bn at Wells Fargo**. These are external forecasts, not Alphabet guidance or booked sales. The spread argues against selecting a single estimate for a forward valuation without reconciling scope and recognition assumptions. [CNBC’s October 28 reporting](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/google-alters-cloud-playbook-turning-to-partners-to-sell-more-ai-chips.html).

For future comparisons, use normalized margins and cash returns after investment, including economically similar financing obligations. Maintain separate scenarios for equipment sales and continuing cloud-service revenue to avoid counting the same capacity twice.
