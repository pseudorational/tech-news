## 6. Industry fundamentals and competition

**Analyst framework:** Enterprise AI adoption should be evaluated through an explicit bridge from workflow improvement to customer economics: deployment expense, recurring inference cost, labor savings, retention and cash collection. A project count or budgeted benefit alone cannot establish return on invested capital. Hg’s earlier disclosure supports the existence of implementation activity, without resolving those economics.

The satellite episode illustrates a different concentration issue: technical availability and permitted use can diverge. Alternative networks could gain attention, but demand for alternatives does not demonstrate their capacity, performance or competitive profitability. No market-share shift is assumed.
